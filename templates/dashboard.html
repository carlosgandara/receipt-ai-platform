💡 How the Refresh Token Works

If your Access Token expires (e.g., after 1 hour), the frontend automatically catches the 401 error, calls /refresh to get new tokens, and retries the request—all without you noticing.

Try it: Set JWT_EXPIRATION = datetime.timedelta(minutes=1) in your config, log in, wait 1 minute, and click "Refresh Data". You'll see it silently recover.
